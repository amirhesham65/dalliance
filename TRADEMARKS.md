# Trademarks

The code in this repository is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The name and look of the product are not. "Dalliance", the Dalliance logo and the app icon are trademarks of Amir Hesham Ibrahim. Section 6 of the license says the same: it grants no rights to them.

This page says what you can do with them. The aim is simple: people should know when they're using Dalliance and when they're using something built from it.

## Fine without asking

- Use the name to refer to the project, accurately. "Works with Dalliance notebooks", "a fork of Dalliance" and "based on Dalliance" are all fine.
- Build and run Dalliance from this repository, changed or not, for yourself or inside your organization.
- Write about Dalliance, teach it, and show screenshots of it.
- Link to the project with its name.

## Needs written permission

- Shipping or hosting a changed version under the name Dalliance, or with its logo or icon.
- Using the name, logo or icon in your product's name, domain, app store listing or company name.
- Using a name or logo close enough to Dalliance's to be confused with it.
- Anything that suggests Dalliance made, endorses or supports your product when it doesn't.

## If you fork

Pick a new name and replace the marks:

- the app icon, `apps/desktop/build/icon.png`
- the logo drawn on the start page and in the setup, in `apps/desktop/src/components/welcome/`
- the website's mark, wordmark and icons: `apps/web/src/lib/mark.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/Wordmark.tsx`, `apps/web/src/app/icon.svg`, `favicon.ico` and `apple-icon.tsx`

Keep the LICENSE and NOTICE files, and say that your project is based on Dalliance if you like.

## Asking

Open an issue on this repository to ask for permission or to report misuse.
